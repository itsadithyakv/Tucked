# What's in Tucked — Phase 1 (for supervisors, not developers)

**The room device does the day's paperwork as you work.**
Sign children in and out by swiping their card — arrivals ask the two-second "how do they seem?" question the regulation requires, and sign-outs only complete against the authorised pickup list, with restricted persons blocked outright. Live ratio for the room, with the reduced-ratio windows applied correctly and volunteers never counted. Nap boards prompt sleep checks at your policy interval and refuse checks that wouldn't be legal to record. Meals (one tap for the whole room), diapers, notes, medication from the child's authorisations — blanket items included — and a full accident report form that automatically alerts the family. If a child hasn't arrived by your cut-off, the device tells you to call and records the outcome. Everything works offline and syncs itself; the evacuation screen never needs the internet at all.

**Families get calm.**
One short story per child per day, written like a note from the educator — not a feed. Loud notifications exist only for the things that matter now (illness, accidents), and they stay on the parent's screen until acknowledged — which is exactly the "evidence the parent received a copy" the Ministry asks about. Parents complete the enrolment record from home, item by item, with optional consents that are genuinely optional. Messages show exactly who will read them.

**The console answers inspectors in seconds.**
Attendance, daily written records (drafted automatically every morning, closed by a named person), accident reports with acknowledgement evidence, the medication log, sleep checks, every child's 11-item record, and staff files with credential expiries flagged before they lapse. Each register downloads as CSV or prints clean. One button prepares the exact subset the medical officer of health may copy — and nothing more.

**What can never happen.**
Records can't be edited or deleted — corrections are new entries naming who, when and why. Every regulated entry is signed with a staff PIN. Data lives in Canada.

*Not in this release: menus, billing, serious-occurrence filing helper, immunisation tracking, the parent handbook builder — those are next.*
