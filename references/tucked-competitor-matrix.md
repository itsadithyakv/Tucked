## What this says

1. **The five US/NZ incumbents all have the same three modules:** feed, check-in, billing. Parity there is table stakes and is not a reason to switch.
2. **Nobody owns "calm."** Every feed is a firehose by default. Tucked's Now/Later split and one daily story is a real, defensible difference.
3. **Nobody owns Ontario's paperwork.** Lillio and Storypark are closest (sleep checks, incident reports, compliance checklists), but none auto-drafts the daily written record, runs the serious-occurrence clock, or ships an expiry ledger and inspection binder as the front door.
4. **Canadian money is unserved.** Interac e-Transfer reconciliation, pre-authorised debit without parent fees, City subsidy splits, CWELCC caps, and CRA receipts — only Mitten.care touches one of these, and it's a one-centre BC project.
5. **Storypark is the quiet threat in Canada**, not Brightwheel: endorsed by the national federation, provincial deals, French, compliance records. Treat Storypark and Lillio as the two you must win against in Toronto demos.
6. **Deliberately skip**: payroll, lesson-plan marketplaces, CRM/lead nurture, marketing websites, live cameras, US subsidy logic. Those are where incumbents spend and where Toronto centres (full, fee-capped, non-profit-heavy) don't need help.

## Pricing position for Tucked

- **Published, flat, CAD, per enrolled child per month**, with a small-centre minimum, no per-staff fee, no setup fee, no parent-side payment fee, annual discount for non-profits.
- Sit in the same band as the reported Lillio/Brightwheel numbers (roughly $2–4 per child on top of a base, or $6–10 per child flat) but *visible on the website* — the transparency itself is the marketing.
- **Free forever:** the staff credential expiry ledger and compliance calendar for any Ontario centre, no Tucked subscription required. That is the wedge; the app is the upsell.
- **Home child care agencies:** per active home per month.
